About Shivam

I’m a third-year Computer Science student at NFUT, Riverton, and I spend most of my time architecting platforms that other people depend on — a bill-discounting marketplace moving real money, a campus super-app replacing ten fragmented tools, a chain-of-custody system tracking individual garments through a laundry chain.

Two of those systems now run companies; one runs a society I founded and preside over.

I care deeply about correctness guarantees, clean state machines, and systems that hold under load — not just demos that impress in a pitch.

Interests — System architecture · Platform engineering · AI/ML pipelines · Open source · Distributed systems · Developer tooling · Hackathons · EdTech
