About Shivam

I’m a third-year Computer Science student at NFUT, Riverton, and I spend most of my time architecting platforms that other people depend on — a bill-discounting marketplace moving real money, a campus super-app replacing ten fragmented tools, a chain-of-custody system tracking individual garments through a laundry chain.
